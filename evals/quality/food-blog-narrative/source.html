Learning to bake rye on humid days

The dough needed twenty grams less water during a week of heavy rain. That small change restored the shape of the loaf.

Method

  1. Mix until no dry flour remains.
  2. Rest for thirty minutes.
  3. Cool the loaf for two full hours before slicing.

What changed

A longer cooling period made the crumb firm without making it dry.
