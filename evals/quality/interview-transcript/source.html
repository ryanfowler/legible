A conversation about urban trees

Interviewer: What makes a street tree resilient?

Arun Bell: Soil volume matters more than trunk size. Roots need connected space, oxygen, and water that drains slowly.

Interviewer: Which maintenance step has the largest effect?

Arun Bell: Protect the root zone during nearby construction.

Interviewer: Why do young trees fail even when crews water them regularly?

Arun Bell: Water cannot correct compacted soil. A planting pit can act like a pot when the surrounding ground has been compressed by equipment. Roots circle inside the loose soil instead of extending under the pavement where they can support a mature crown.

Interviewer: What should a city measure after planting?

Arun Bell: Measure canopy growth, soil moisture at several depths, and the condition of the root flare. Survival alone is a weak measure. A tree can remain alive for years without gaining the structure it needs to shade a street.
